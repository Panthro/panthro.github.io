Upgrid is a Swiss energy-community platform — a marketplace where local solar producers sell directly to neighbors under ZEV, LEG, and vZEV rules. No hardware install, no intermediaries: lower bills and locally sourced renewable energy.

As co-founder and CTO I built the technical foundation from scratch: SDAT metering ingestion, fifteen-minute consumption allocation, a billing engine with Swiss QR-bill PDFs, Bexio accounting sync, and operator workflows for community formation and invoice management. The platform serves consumers, full operator accounts, co-brand partners (municipalities and installers), and affiliates — each with different permissions and workflows on the same codebase.

The engineering bet is a monorepo for a small team in a regulated domain — infrastructure as code, feature-flag governance, CI ratchets, and a company brain agent so ops can self-serve without routing every question through engineering. I also own product and go-to-market work: savings calculator, LEG content, PostHog funnel instrumentation, and multi-channel acquisition.
