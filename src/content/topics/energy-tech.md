Swiss energy law opened Lokale Energiegemeinschaften in 2025, but the hard part isn't marketing solar panels — it's the platform behind them. Metering data arrives on grid schedules. Allocation runs on fifteen-minute intervals. Invoices carry Swiss QR-bill references and sync to accounting systems. Get any of that wrong and you have support tickets, recalculation jobs, and angry operators — not just a broken signup form.

Upgrid is a Swiss energy-community marketplace connecting local solar producers with neighbors: lower prices, local renewable energy, no hardware install. The technical work spans regulated billing and allocation, full operator accounts, co-brand partnerships with municipalities and installers, affiliate attribution, and the go-to-market instrumentation to measure whether any of it converts.

This hub collects writing on energy tech as it accumulates. [Building Upgrid](/articles/building-upgrid/) is the primary deep dive — architecture multipliers, the money path from SDAT to invoice, and what it means to apply a fintech correctness bar to community energy. The [Upgrid work entry](/work/upgrid/) covers the role and scope at a glance.
